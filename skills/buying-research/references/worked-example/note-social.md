# Instagram and Facebook: electric scooters (read 31 Oct 2026)

*Sample note, condensed. It shows how to read social media, and how to say that it did not change much.*

Read in the buyer's logged-in Chrome, with their consent. Read-only: nothing was liked, followed, joined, commented on or sent. Everything is paraphrased. No names, handles, phone numbers, vehicle numbers or post links to private people are recorded.

Tags: **paid** labelled partnership or #Ad. **dealer** dealer or brand post. **creator** channel that may have commercial links. **owner** an ordinary comment or post.

## What could and could not be read
### Takeaway
Yield was mostly short complaints. About 25 Instagram posts and 25 Facebook searches and feeds gave about 65 items, many of them one line.
### Cited Findings
| Area | Read | Not read, and why |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram reels | Caption and first-loaded comments on 25 posts | **The videos themselves.** Claims inside reels are unknown |
| Facebook groups, public | Post bodies from several owner groups | Most comments. Many posts had no visible date |
| Facebook groups, private | Nothing | Not joined. The largest owner group is private, so its absence matters |
| Dealer pages | Search listings only | Reviews |

### Inferences
One group's own admin says it over-represents people with problems. That is fair, and it applies to all of these.
