# Owners: Ather Rizta (read 31 Oct 2026)

*Sample note, condensed. It shows how to report owner evidence honestly.*

How to read this:
- Counts are accounts read, **not failure rates**. Unhappy owners post more.
- Accounts are paraphrased. No names, phone numbers or registration numbers are recorded.
- Tags: [read] page text. [auto-captions] YouTube transcript, video not watched, numbers may be garbled. [snippet] search summary only.
- Km, cost and outcome are what the owner says. None of it is verified.
