# India pack

**Tested on:** two-wheelers, an AC, a laptop and a ghar ghanti. Details for other categories are less proven.

## Tier guide

Quick: under Rs 25,000. Standard: Rs 25,000 to Rs 75,000. Deep: above Rs 75,000, or a loan on anything above Rs 25,000. The user can change it.

## Format

Show money as Rs 1,49,042 and use lakh for large amounts. Hindi or Hinglish messages get replies in the same style, with model names and numbers unchanged.

## Prices

- **Vehicles:** on-road price is what counts. Get the full breakup: ex-showroom, subsidy, road tax and RTO, insurance, and other charges. Check that it adds up. Prices vary by state and city, so set the site to the buyer's city.
- **Appliances and electronics:** compare the price with delivery, installation and any compulsory kit (such as AC stand and extra copper pipe). Compare with exchange and without it.
- Check whether GST and any scheme or subsidy are already included, or may have ended.
- Festive periods are common, but count an offer only if a dated one was found.

## Finance

- **Flat or reducing:** many offers quote flat rates, which sound much lower. Always check with `scripts/loan.py check`.
- **Fees:** processing fee, and any GST on it, count in the true rate.
- **"No-cost EMI":** it is a bank loan with the interest handed back as a discount. Check the fee, GST on interest and any lost cash discount with `scripts/loan.py no-cost`.
- **GST:** card EMI interest carries GST, while ordinary loan interest appears exempt on secondary sources, so confirm before comparing routes. Add it with the `no-cost` command, or by hand for ordinary card EMI.
- **Quotes behind a login:** online purchases rarely show public EMI totals. Give illustrated cases, then tell the buyer which two numbers to ask for: the lowest pay-in-full price, and the EMI total with GST and fees.
- **Very low rates** may need a large down payment and a short tenure. Read the terms.
- **Key Fact Statement:** ask the lender for it. It sets out the true rate and fees. Card EMI is exempt, so read the EMI screen instead.
- **Prepayment:** ask whether the loan is fixed or floating, and what foreclosure costs.
- Advise keeping formal applications to one or two, since each may be recorded on the buyer's credit report.

## Category checks

- **ACs:**
  - Model suffixes can change the specs, so check the exact suffix.
  - "1.5 ton" can mean different rated watts, so compare the watts.
  - Sizing rules from sellers run high. Show them against a basic heat estimate and say whether the pick survives both.
  - Bedroom use makes indoor and outdoor noise a deciding factor. Minimum running capacity also matters at night, where the maker publishes it.
  - Star labels changed in January 2026 and differ by model year, so compare ISEER, not stars or label kWh.
  - Check whether non-authorised installation voids the warranty, and whether a stabiliser is required. Tell the buyer to ask for a vacuum pump at installation.
- **Laptops:**
  - Memory: soldered, memory-on-package or SODIMM, and slot count, which can differ under one model name. Price any later upgrade the plan depends on.
  - Compare by spec as well as part number, since one machine can carry different numbers per channel. Check a maker's family page sorted by price before calling a price gap real.
  - Check the shipped battery Wh, screen nits and colour, keyboard backlight, and whether the base warranty is on-site or carry-in.
  - Treat listings with more memory than any maker build with suspicion. Who fitted it, and does the maker's warranty survive?
- **Electronics generally:** check that the warranty has not already started, using the serial on the maker's site, inside the return window. Battery cover is often shorter than system cover. Check that an add-on plan applies to this exact product line.
- **Home goods such as ghar ghanti:** check the floor or counter space, whether the buyer's current way of doing this is cheaper, and the BIS or ISI mark where applicable.

| Appliances | Maker's site and store, BEE star label, Amazon.in, Flipkart, Croma, Reliance Digital, Vijay Sales, TechEnclave |

| Authorised service | [Dell](https://www.dell.com/support/diagnose/en-in/servicecenter), [HP](https://support.hp.com/in-en/help/service-center), [Lenovo](https://support.lenovo.com/in/en/lenovo-service-provider/), and each brand's own finder |
| Complaints | MouthShut, Consumer Complaints, app store reviews |
| Lenders | Lender's schedule of charges, bank rate pages |

Without Chrome, Amazon.in, Flipkart, Croma, Justdial, Team-BHP, Reddit and the Dell and Lenovo stores often fail. Some maker stores, Reliance Digital product pages, Vijay Sales, TechEnclave, bank sites, and price trackers such as PriceHistory and PriceBefore have worked, but tracker prices may not name the exact model suffix. Maker document hosts such as download.lenovo.com often work when the main site does not. Do not count affiliate "best of" sites or sellers' own blogs as evidence.

## Local fit

Consider what changes the answer here: voltage swings, summer heat, humidity and pests, hard water, monsoon flooding and storage, and service reach beyond large cities. YouTube and Facebook reviews are often in Hindi or regional languages, so read those too, and note that auto-captions may garble them.
