# Report template

Save as `report.md`. Write in the buyer's language, in plain words. Quick reports may be a page or so: leave out any section that does not apply, but always keep **What we could not read**. Deep reports use every section that applies.

```markdown
# <The verdict as a title, e.g. "Buy the X, lock its battery cover">

**Pick: <exact model and variant, price, date, place>. Confidence: <High, Medium or Low>.**
**Fallback: <exact model and price>.**
<A short paragraph: why it wins, on what, by how much; its main weaknesses; what being wrong would cost; what must happen before paying.>
<If confidence is Low, the one check that would settle it, and which way each answer points.>

**How to read this.** <Explain only the labels this report uses: [Official] maker's or lender's page, [Retail] seller or price tracker, [Test] independent test, [Offer] a quote for you, [Owner] unverified owner story, [Ours] our calculation or judgement, [summary] and [snippet] page not read word for word.> Complaint counts are not failure rates. Prices as of <date>.

## <What decides this, stated as a finding>
Your arithmetic and the deciding factors. A table of every serious option, on the same basis, with a one-line verdict each.

## The pick's weaknesses, plainly
| Weakness | Evidence | What it means for you |

## What would flip the pick
Concrete conditions, and what to choose then.

## What it costs over the time you keep it
Price, running costs, loan cost, likely repairs. Label estimates. Say what is left out.

## Finance
Include the line: "This is comparison and arithmetic, not licensed financial advice. Confirm terms with the lender."
Table of EMI, fees, total cost and true rate. Say which numbers are real quotes and which are illustrations.

## Where to buy and get service
Service evidence first. Say which listings were rating only. For items replaced rather than repaired, cover only the seller and returns or statutory rights.

## What owners report
Findings by theme, with how much weight each deserves.

## What else we checked
Every other contender, each with a reason it is out.

## Corrections
What was earlier said that turned out wrong, and what is true now.

## Before paying
A short plan, and for bigger purchases, exact questions to ask the seller in writing. Say whether any dated offer was found.

## What we could not read
Platforms and pages not read, and why. Logins the buyer declined. Whether any bill, odometer or claim was verified.

## Sources
Link official sources next to claims where possible. Do not link to individual people's posts where the link would identify them.
```

## Standards

- The first paragraph must be enough on its own to act on.
- Numbers carry a date, a place and a label.
- Show the same variant name everywhere.
- Never name reviewers or record their phone or vehicle numbers. Paraphrase, and keep any quote short.
